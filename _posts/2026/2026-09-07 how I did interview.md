# How I did interview to be efficient
> My experience receive interview and did interview

## My background
I'm a software technical lead. In my role I got involved to do interview if the candidate will be a team member with me.
Although sometime not limited to, as if the candidate came from the country as me, I will also help as backup translator.

As the candidate, I also experienced in receiving interview. 
Which this post will cover how it feels on both side.

## What company I look for
I prefer to apply job on company where the company size is less than 100 people, the less the better. 
Because it's easier to get close to manager, CEO or decision maker directly, where the company size still at this range.
The reason I want to get close is to understand the business goals, culture rather than just receiving tasks, which help me being proactive & grow to achieve the goals.
Help to shape how to improve process works as my voice is easier to heard here.
And most of time, amazed with how they think to run the business which it something that I want to learn.

The business at this size also tend to have less steps of interviews as they value efficiency.

If you think working with bigger company is safer & stable because of higher capital or market reach.
THINK AGAIN! I just read news where big company just layoff 90% of their staffs.
It's the small to medium size company that I found scale their team carefully. 

## How I did interview
When I did interview, I limit it into 3 steps. 
The reason is efficiency and I didn't want to burden the candidates. 
I put myself on their shoes, treat others as how you want to be treated. 

Will you enjoy 10 steps inteviews meeting with 10 different people and gain their approval?

**1st step** 
Video call introduction. The goal is for us to see the ability to communicate each other, attitude, work culture and
inform what is the next step if pass. At this stage we have cleared expectation of compensation.

When I did interview, it's important for me to be accompanied by my manager or someone above me as peer how I did
interview. If not possible, I will ask to do video recording to be reviewed later. The reason for this is to make sure
I did interview in a good manner, representing the company & its culture correctly. If I decided the person pass or did
not pass the interview, I will ask my manager if my decision correct or not.

It's important for me to do interview happen face to face before the next step as I respect the candidate.
Respect goes both ways. 

**2nd step**
On this step usually max 5 candidates are selected, I gave a written instruction for coding task to take home. I gave a
minimum requirements which basically proof they can do the job, with time limit 1 week where the task most likely take
less than 6 hours, now with AI can be done less than 1 hour.

The task like:
Build APIs that capable to do CRUD operation and one of it have a function that could take 5 minutes to process.
Do your best to make the function can run stable with the x framework. Where x is the framework we use at work.

I did not ask for leet-code or similar proctored test, I have tried that, it didn't work in my case. There was case where
the person that I recommend at that time failed the test that require me to convince my manager disregard that test and
use simpler approach that simulate a real work environment. We're not looking for rocket engineer, but someone
who can manage the task from simple brief. That candidate end up have proven better than our expectation and promoted.

I did not do face to face code review, system design, etc. I understand the candidate might be **nervous** during the call
that made them forgot things they have learnt/did or unable to communicate it better. Doing this kind of interview
will also cost me a lot time multiplied by the quantity of candidates. Imagine yourself asking the same thing over and over
to different persons.

The result of the take home test most of time are surprising. Some are using dependency injection, attribute/decorator,
implemented SOLID, DRY, KISS and created a fork function since the framework current version did not support the feature, which
show their deep understanding of the framework. 

Regardless if they are using Google or AI to do it, they have proven they can do it. If they didn't have the skill to do
it before, they have proven able to learn fast which also amazing.

Remember, in remote work environment, the engineer are doing the job alone, the task goal are to simulate that situation.

**3rd step**
If they pass the code review in 2nd steps, I might ask to invite for another interview to ask about their decision
on architectured the task. The goals is to make sure they understand what the code they produce.

At this steps, I usually already have 1 strong candidate. We offer probation period which it 6 months were we will
monitor closely, make sure he/she can do the job, have weekly meeting for KPI. 

I expect them to have something to learn & adapt, we're hiring for long-term not short-time freelance.
The candidate that already know everyting most likely to leave early as there is no room for growth anymore. 

## How many job posting required to hire?
Only **once**, if they pass the trial tasks which prove can do the job. I will always choose someone or more to follow the business need.
I'm not looking for **perfect human being that did not exist**. 
I prefer to be the person that have choosen someone and I'm wrong, rather than keep guessing I'm right and keep looking. 
There will be probation period and we will not give all access during this time which reduce the risk.

## How is my recent inteview experience?
After 2+ months looking for job, I got some interviews, lot of rejection letters and 1 ghosted.

**One that amazed me**
It's a company from USA, small company with less than 15 people. Been stable for more than decade.
I interviewed directly with the founder. What most interesting is, the interviewer didn't ask me about skill, programming languages,
etc. I guess he trust me I have it all, otherwise will be proven by the trial task. He more interested with my communication & problem solver skill.

We have video call introduction, then list set of questions asked which I reply later. This show that he have mastered
the art of async communincations as all question as asked at once in detail. I replied, pass and went to the next step.

I given paid trial task, which it a sign this is fair company. He more interested in how I will communicate about this task,
solve it, discuss trade-off of the choosen approach.

I didn't pass the interview, it was close call, both candidates are great. 
We're still connected, in case there are future opening for me.

**Headhunters or Talent Hunters**
This is the first time I interviewed by headhunter. I guess with how AI now mass applied at jobs opening and company ATS
system incorrectly flagged applicants, there will companies that prefer to have headhunter to look for quality talent for them.

I have 2 headhunters that already interviewed me. Both are great and discussed good compensation early.
But no decision yet as the company that hired them still busy and not available for the next step.
And they have informed the interview steps is small like my 3 steps above.

**Ghosted**
If we already have interview face-to-face, it's better we informed the candidate if they pass or not. Leaving us ghosted will
damage the company reputation. I may not be hired, but if I leave with good impression, there is chance where I will
recommend the company product to my connections.
