## Nothing is permanent

## Start investing early
When we got our paycheck, first thing came to mind is what I can bought with it?
Which vacation destination should I visit?
Or purchasing something with debt because my paycheck is enough to cover the instalment?

DON'T!
Instead I will invest it to get yield from it and only the yield will be used to purchase something that is not a necessity.
vacation, dinning, entertainment, luxury items.

Formula of saving from 100% paycheck with target yield minimum 10% annually.

**Minimum plan**
50% for cost of living
50% for passive investment

with this formula, saving 50% of paycheck, I will be financial independent within **10 years** as it will cover my necessities.

**Ideal plan**
30% for cost of living
60% for passive investment
10% for active investment

with this formula, financial independence will be achieved within **5 years!**
